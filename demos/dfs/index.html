DSC 40B – Theoretical Foundations of Data Science II

Depth-First Search

Instructions: Choose a graph and a source node, then click the buttons (or press the ← and → keys) to step through a depth-first search. Each step ends when dfs is called or returns. As in lecture, each node is undiscovered, pending, or visited, and a node's neighbors are produced in sorted order.

The tree of function calls shows the nested calls to dfs, where dfs(u) is short for dfs(graph, u, status). Each call is indented under the call that made it. The calls that haven't returned yet are pending; they are the call stack. The call that is running is circled, both in the graph and in the tree, and the edges it explores are drawn in bold.

Check Start and finish times to show the clock above the graph, and each node's [start, finish] times both above the node in the graph and next to its call in the tree. The clock goes up by one whenever a node is marked pending or visited; the demo shows the tick just before the node changes color.
